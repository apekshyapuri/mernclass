Trave and tourism in Nepal

Nepal is a beautiful country known for its majestic mountains, diverse landscapes, rich culture, ancient heritage, and warm hospitality. From the Himalayas and peaceful lakes to historic temples, national parks, and traditional villages, Nepal offers a wide range of experiences for travelers. It is also famous for trekking, mountaineering, wildlife exploration, cultural tours, and adventure activities.
